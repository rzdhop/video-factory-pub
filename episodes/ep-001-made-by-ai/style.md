# ep-001-made-by-ai — art direction

Look: `looks/video-factory.json` (ink ground, paper shapes, ONE vermilion accent). Medium: a printed poster.
Flat solid shapes, hard edges. No light, no glow, no particles, no gradient, no blur, no soft shadow (all switched off in the look).

1. One world: a small film set that is also a factory floor, seen from the side, printed like a two-colour poster: paper shapes on ink, a paper floor line, a conveyor.
2. The object we follow: THIS video, drawn as a vertical rounded frame (the frame of the logo) that travels down the line and gains one part per station: caption bars, a check badge, the play triangle. Complete, it is the project logo.
3. Accent: vermilion (`tk.color.accent`) sits on ONE thing per shot, the thing the voice is talking about: the backrest marked AI of the empty chair at frame 0 and on the last frame, the part just added to the frame, the mark of the ask counter that just filled, the YES button. Everything else is paper (`tk.color.text`) or a paper tint (`tk.color.steel`, `tk.color.pink`). The host wears a flat mid-ink top and has flat fills (look keys `character.top`, `character.flat`), so he never competes with it. ONE exception, in the question chapter: the logo keeps its own two accent parts (the play triangle and one caption bar: it is the logo), so from "chair" to the loop there are two accents on screen, the backrest and the logo. The extras of that chapter (book, MIT tag, second frame) are drawn in paper or a paper tint, never in the accent.
4. Light: none is simulated. Depth comes from overlap only (the clapperboard in front of the belt, the far legs of the chair in a paper tint).
5. Camera: the frame stays put and the stations come to it along the belt; a punch on the accent words; a dead stop on "stops".
6. On screen all episode: the floor line, the belt and the frame on it. The ask counter (the word ASKS and three marks, top left of the scene box) arrives on "three times" and stays until the third ask. The director's chair is on screen in the first and in the last chapter.
7. Signature transition: `whip-left`, the belt carries the frame out and the next station in; `zoom` for the last chapter, back to the whole set.
8. Shared parts live in `src/layers/scenes/Ep001_shared.tsx` (orchestrator only): `geo` (the one layout), `Floor`, `Belt`, `DirectorChair` (empty, AI on the backrest), `Clapper`, `VideoFrame` (the travelling frame, parts switched on one by one), `Logo` (the project logo, redrawn from the SVG source), `AskCounter`, `Hand` (the person: it always comes in from the right edge, never across the HUD; the stamp arm of the second agent hangs from a mount at the top of the scene box, under the HUD), `Sign`, `Check`, `YesButton`, `Barrier`, `MitTag`, `Book`, `Word`. Every colour from `kit(preset)`.
9. Text in scenes: one face (Space Grotesk bold, `Word`), upper case for signs: AI, ASKS, IDEAS, SCRIPT, FACT CHECK, VOICE, CODE, SCORE, STRONG, RENDER, YES, MIT, and the project name `video-factory`. Never the narrator's sentence. Nothing to read in the bottom quarter of the scene box.
10. Calm technical staging (format sheet): at most 2-3 elements at a time, one station on screen at a time, each reveal on its word; music 90 bpm at -19 dB.

Chapters:
- hook (`ep001-hook`): frame 0 = the empty set, complete: the director's chair with AI on its backrest in the accent and nobody in it, the empty frame of this video on the belt, the clapperboard open in front. "Almost": the clapper snaps shut, the chair hops. "had to ask": a question mark in a bubble comes out of the frame. "a person": a paper hand comes in from the right. "three": the ask counter arrives with three empty marks.
- line (`ep001-line`): the frame rides past six stations, one per phrase. IDEAS: three cards on "pitched"; on "picked one" the hand takes the middle card by its edge (the card turns to the accent), carries it over the frame and drops it in, mark 1 fills. SCRIPT: the caption bars appear in the frame. FACT CHECK: on "agent" a second arm comes down with a stamp, on "fact" it stamps a check badge on the frame. VOICE: a big question mark on "asked again", the hand comes back on "paying", on "for the voice" the voice bars play and mark 2 fills. CODE: the play triangle on "drew", angle brackets around the frame on "code". SCORE: a meter with a sleeping needle on "hear"; the needle hunts on "picks sounds"; at the start of "by their score" it lands in the accent zone and STRONG is written, held about 0.9 s before the cut.
- human (`ep001-human`): the finished frame rides under the RENDER sign while its progress bar fills; on "stops" the belt stops dead in front of a barrier (the one close-up of the host). "Last question": the YES button comes out of the floor in the accent, a question mark over it, nobody to press it. "may I": mark 3 fills and the hand comes in from the right; it presses the button, the barrier lifts and the frame leaves.
- question (`ep001-question`): the set of frame 0 again, same places: the chair (backrest in paper), the clapperboard, and the project logo where the empty frame stood. "engine": the logo answers. "rulebook": a book (paper tint) lands on the seat of the chair. "open source": the MIT tag (paper) drops on the corner of the logo. "yours": the book is gone, the clapperboard steps aside and a second, empty frame in paper stands in its place. "Would you": it leaves and the clapperboard is back. "chair": the accent is back on the backrest marked AI, the chair hops. "GitHub": the name `video-factory` is written over the logo. Last image = first image: the empty chair marked AI, the clapperboard, the belt, with the logo in place of the empty frame.
