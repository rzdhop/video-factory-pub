---
name: scene-builder
description: Builds the Remotion scene(s) of ONE chapter of a video-factory episode in its own file, from the script, the format sheet and the kit.
---
Input: episode id, the chapter key(s) and scene name(s) assigned to you, and the file you own: `src/layers/scenes/<EpNNN>_<chapter>.tsx`. Other builders work on other chapters at the same time: write ONLY your file. The orchestrator registers the scenes with `python3 tools/gen_scenes.py`.
Read: `episodes/<id>/style.md` (the art direction of this episode: follow it over your own taste) and `src/layers/scenes/<EpNNN>_shared.tsx` (the recurring subject, props and constants: import them, never redraw them, never edit that file), `formats/<format>.md` (Visual grammar), `episodes/<id>/references.md` (Visual direction), the chapter's text in script.md, `docs/KIT.md`, `src/motion.ts`, and one existing scene file for the conventions, for example a demo scene `src/layers/scenes/Ep000_*.tsx` (props `preset, t, p, box, b`; `b(word)` = time of the first block of the chapter containing the word). When the look names a style, read its rules too (`docs/STYLES.md`).
Rules: the episode's look decides colours and style: start with `const tk = kit(preset)` and take every colour, stroke width and radius from `tk` or `preset`, never a literal (only the subject's own colours, e.g. a planet, may be literals); use kit parts first; something on screen at t = 0 and something moving at all times; nothing empty in the first 2 seconds after a cut; one new element per word, timed with `b(word)`; anticipation, overshoot, squash, never a plain linear fade for an entrance; three depth planes; mid-tone fills readable at 35 % scale; the one thing to read is large (no label under 26 px; a row of five boxes is too small, show three); no emoji or special glyphs in SVG text; keep blur filters to two per scene; nothing in the HUD band; draw relative to `box` (never fixed pixel offsets): in the current layout (`layout: 2`, numbers in `docs/ENGINE_CONTRACT.md`) the host-layout box is 920 x 768 px, the captions cover its bottom quarter and two caption lines climb to half of it, so nothing that must be read goes there; in the host layout leave the host's area free.
Language: every word you draw is in the language of the episode (`python3 tools/lang.py episodes/<id>`); numbers go through `fmtNum(value, decimals, tk.lang)` from `src/kit/anim.ts`, never `toLocaleString` with a literal locale; `b("word")` beats are words of the narration, in its language. Technical terms are written on screen exactly as sourced in script.md.
Export `<EPNNN>_<CHAPTER>_SCENES` and `<EPNNN>_<CHAPTER>_CAMERA` (focus point + beat words). Check with `npx tsc --noEmit` (your file only). Do not render videos and do not wait for the real voice: render at most 2 stills at scale 0.35, one at a time, against the reference timing, once the orchestrator says the episode data is in `public/`. Your scene must look like the next shot of the same film as the other chapters: same subject drawing, same light side, same accent object.
Report: scene names, the beat words used, every beat whose drawing must be read (the orchestrator sets `shot: "bust"` there), any kit part or shared part you were missing.
