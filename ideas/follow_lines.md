# Follow lines already used (docs/RETENTION.md §4)

The follow call (at one third of the video) and the end line never use the same sentence in two episodes. Write the
two sentences of each new episode here, newest first, and read the last five before writing new ones.

The promise itself never changes: it is what the brand really posts, at its real rhythm (`content` and
`weekly_target` of the brand in `brands/brands.json`). Only the wording changes. Never "one video a day on <this
episode's subject>" unless that is true.

Rules when writing a new pair:
- 3 s at most for the line at one third, placed after a payoff;
- a new angle each time: what the viewer becomes by following, what the channel refuses to do, a real earlier video;
- the end line names ONE place to go, and only a place that exists when the video goes out (write it in brackets);
- two episodes of the same week do not send to the same earlier video under two different names.
